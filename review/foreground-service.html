Foreground Service Demonstration

Android app version 2.0.0. The recording shows real controls from the signed application; the capture is cropped to the relevant controls.

  1. 0–4 seconds: the equalizer is initially off.
  2. 4–8 seconds: the user enables the equalizer. The special-use foreground service starts.
  3. 8–15 seconds: Android’s app-info screen is in front. The equalizer foreground service continues running.
  4. 15–19 seconds: returning to the equalizer preserves its enabled state.
  5. 19–26 seconds: the user disables the equalizer. Audio effects and the foreground service stop.

No microphone audio was recorded. Foreground service start, continued background operation and stop were also verified against Android’s running-service state. No login is required to view this recording.
